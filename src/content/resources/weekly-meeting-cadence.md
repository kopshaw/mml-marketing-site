The single most leveraged operating habit a $1M–$25M business can install is a disciplined weekly leadership meeting. It's also the one most operators get wrong.

## The standard agenda

Run this same agenda every week. The discipline is the system.

| Block | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Check-in** | 5 min | Each leader: one personal/business win + one stuck point. Sets the room. |
| **2. Scorecard review** | 15 min | Each leader's scorecard numbers shown vs. target. On-track: 30 sec. Off-track: → issues list. |
| **3. Rock review** | 15 min | 90-day Rocks status: on track / off track / done. Off-track Rocks → issues list. |
| **4. Headlines** | 5 min | Customer + employee headlines worth flagging. Information only. |
| **5. To-do review** | 5 min | Last week's to-dos: done / not done. Not-done → today's to-dos or next week. |
| **6. Issues list** | 30 min | IDS: Identify (state the issue), Discuss (5 min max), Solve (decision + owner + due date). |
| **7. Conclude** | 5 min | Recap to-dos. Cascade messages. Rate the meeting 1–10. |

Total: 80 minutes. Builds in to 60–90 with discipline.

## What kills the meeting

Three habits kill weekly leadership meetings, predictably:

1. **Drift.** No agenda or no enforcement of agenda. Discussions take whatever time the loudest leader gives them.

2. **Resolution avoidance.** Issues get discussed but not decided. They reappear the next week. After 4 weeks, the leadership team learns the meeting is theater, not decision-making.

3. **Founder dominance.** The founder facilitates, drives every conversation, and answers every question. The team learns to wait for the founder to speak. Six months in, the meeting can't run without the founder.

## What actually works

The fix is structural, not cultural:

- **Same time, same place, same attendees.** Calendar lock. No reschedules.
- **Rotating facilitator.** After three months, the founder is not the default facilitator. Different leader runs the meeting each week.
- **Hard time-boxes.** Issues that need more than 5 minutes get tagged for offline resolution with a named owner and a deadline.
- **Decision capture.** Every issue closed with: *what's the decision, who owns it, by when?* Written down. Reviewed next week.
- **Rate the meeting.** End every meeting with each leader scoring it 1–10. Average below 8 → fix the next one.

## What changes after 90 days

By month three, the cadence should be:

- The team brings issues without prompting
- Decisions are made in the meeting, not after it
- Scorecards are visible to everyone, not surprises
- The founder is one voice in the meeting, not the meeting

After this point, you can run the meeting with the founder absent and lose nothing. That's the real test — and the leverage.
