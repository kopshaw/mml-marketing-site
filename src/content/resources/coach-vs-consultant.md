Operators looking for outside help generally hire one of three things — and most of them buy the wrong one for the problem they actually have.

## Coach

The coaching model: ask questions, help the operator find their own answers, hold them accountable to action.

When it works: when the operator already has the infrastructure to execute and just needs reflection, accountability, or someone to talk through judgment calls with.

When it doesn't: when the operator is missing the actual infrastructure — playbooks, dashboards, scorecards. A coach asking *"what do you think you should do?"* doesn't produce a documented sales process. It produces another conversation.

## Consultant

The consulting model: analyze the business, deliver recommendations, hand off a deliverable (usually a deck or a plan).

When it works: when the operator has the team, the operating discipline, and the time to execute the recommendations on their own — and just needs the strategic direction or expert analysis to point the way.

When it doesn't: when the operator doesn't have the team or the discipline. A great strategic plan executed by a stretched-thin founder with no operating infrastructure produces nothing — the plan goes in the binder and the business reverts to the same patterns.

## Installer

The installer model: build the infrastructure inside the business, embed long enough to make sure it works, hand off when the operator can run it.

When it works: when the gap is *systems*, not *mindset* and not *strategy*. When what's missing is documented playbooks, role scorecards, dashboards, and operating rhythms.

What it produces: documented infrastructure the team uses Monday morning. Not a deck. Not a binder. Working systems.

## How to tell which one you need

Three quick tests:

1. **Can your team articulate your sales process stage-by-stage in writing right now?** If no, you have a systems gap. Installer.

2. **Do you have a documented role scorecard for every key seat, reviewed weekly?** If no, you have a systems gap. Installer.

3. **Can a non-founder team member run last month's financial close from documentation?** If no, you have a systems gap. Installer.

If you can answer yes to all three but still feel stuck on direction or execution, you may have a coaching or consulting need. If you answered no to any of them, more coaching or strategy work won't move the business — installed infrastructure will.
